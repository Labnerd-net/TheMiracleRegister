Miracles Project - Saints & Candidates (Last 50 Years, c. 1976-2026)

CANONIZED SAINTS WITH WELL-DOCUMENTED MIRACLES:

John Paul II (canonized 2014)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Sr. Marie Simon-Pierre (Parkinson's disease, 2005)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Floribeth Mora Diaz (brain aneurysm, Costa Rica, 2011)
- Notes: Pope 1978-2005, canonization miracles well-documented by Vatican medical board

Mother Teresa (canonized 2016)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Monica Besra (Bengal woman, abdominal tumor, 1998)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Marcilio Andrino (Brazilian man, multiple brain abscesses, 2008)
- Notes: 2nd miracle attributed to intercession, confirmed 2015

Padre Pio / Pio of Pietrelcina (canonized 2002)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Consiglia De Martino (stomach rupture, 1950s)
- Miracle 2: Healing of the 'de Matteis' boy (meningitis-to-coma recovery)
- Additional: Many attributed healings, stigmata, bilocation reports
- Notes: Canonized 2002, but died 1968; attributed miracles span decades

Faustina Kowalska (canonized 2000)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Maureen Digan (lymphedema, 1981)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Fr. Ron Pytel (heart condition, 1995)
- Notes: Apostle of Divine Mercy, Diary widely read

John XXIII / Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli (canonized 2014)
- Miracle: Healing of Sr. Caterina Capitani (stomach cancer, 1966)
- Notes: Dispensed from 2nd miracle requirement by Pope Francis; called "Good Pope John"

Josemaria Escriva (canonized 2002)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Dr. Manuel Nevado Rey (chronic radiodermatitis, 1976)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Maria del Carmen Gutierrez (lip cancer) / others
- Notes: Founder of Opus Dei

Gianna Beretta Molla (canonized 2004)
- Miracle: Healing of Sr. Maria Vergine Colombo (breast tumor, healing during Gianna's pregnancy)
- Notes: Laywoman, mother, doctor. Pro-life symbol

Louis and Zelie Martin (canonized 2015)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Carmen (young girl, severe lung condition, 1980s)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Pietro (baby, severe respiratory failure)
- Notes: Parents of St. Therese of Lisieux; first married couple canonized together

Edith Stein / Teresa Benedicta of the Cross (canonized 1998)
- Miracle 1: Healing of young girl from lethal acetaminophen poisoning (1987)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Filipino boy from drug-resistant pneumonia
- Notes: Jewish convert to Catholicism, Carmelite nun, Auschwitz martyr

Maximilian Kolbe (canonized 1982)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Maria Gąsior (intestinal tuberculosis)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Angela Testoni (calcified peritoneum / intestinal disease)
- Notes: Martyr of charity, died at Auschwitz. Canonized by John Paul II

Kateri Tekakwitha (canonized 2012)
- Miracle 1: Healing of a young boy (Jake Finkbonner, flesh-eating bacteria, 2006)
- Miracle 2: Healing of a woman (severe burns, 1960s)
- Notes: First Native American saint

Andre Bessette (canonized 2010)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Joseph Audet (tuberculosis, 1916)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Mario (young boy, tarsal fracture would not heal)
- Notes: Holy Cross brother, known as 'Miracle Man of Montreal', St. Joseph's Oratory

John Neumann (canonized 1977)
- Miracle 1: Healing of Michael (boy, peritonitis from ruptured appendix)
- Miracle 2: Healing of Theresa (woman, tumor)
- Notes: First American bishop canonized

Marguerite d'Youville (canonized 1990)
- Miracle 1: Healing of a woman from leukemia (1970s)
- Miracle 2: Healing from multiple debilitating conditions
- Notes: Foundress of the Grey Nuns

ADDITIONAL CANDIDATES (well-attributed but not yet canonized or very recent):

Carlo Acutis (beatified 2020, canonization expected)
- Miracle 1: Healing of a boy (rare pancreatic disease, Brazil)
- Miracle 2: Second miracle confirmed for canonization (healing of a Costa Rican girl)
- Notes: Patron of the internet, wonderful model for this project

Pier Giorgio Frassati (beatified 1990, canonization expected)
- Miracle 1: Healing of a young man with spinal tuberculosis (1950s)
- Notes: Called 'The Man of the Eight Beatitudes'

Fulton Sheen (venerable, beatification underway)
- Miracle: Healing of James Fulton Engstrom (stillborn baby revived, prayers to Sheen)
- Notes: Popular TV evangelist, bishop

Justino Russolillo (beatified pending)
Numerous healing reports associated with 'apostle of vocations'

Juan Diego (canonized 2002)
- Miracle: Healing of Juan Jose Barragan Silva (catastrophic fall, coma, full recovery in 1989-1990)
- Associated miracle: Tilma of Our Lady of Guadalupe (1531) -- miraculous image imprinted on his cloak
- Notes: Indigenous Mexican saint. Equipollent beatification (dispensed from first miracle due to antiquity of devotion). Tilma is a separate miracle type (apparition/image) from his canonization miracle.
