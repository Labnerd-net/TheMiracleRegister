Miracles Project - Research: Gianna Beretta Molla

Basic Info:
- Born: October 4, 1922, Magenta, Italy
- Died: April 28, 1962, Monza, Italy
- Beatified: April 24, 1994
- Canonized: May 16, 2004 (by John Paul II)
- Feast Day: April 28
- Patronage: Mothers, families, physicians, unborn children

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Sr. Maria Vergine Colombo, a nun in Brazil
- Condition: Breast tumor (malignant, metastasizing)
- Date of healing: Early 1980s
- Location: Brazil
- Key details: The nun prayed to Gianna Beretta Molla (not yet beatified). During a surgery that was supposed to be a mastectomy, the tumor was found to have vanished. Only a biopsy scar remained.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Beatification (1994)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Elizabeth Comparini, a pregnant woman in Brazil
- Condition: Severe placental abruption with hemorrhaging and fetal distress
- Date of healing: 2002
- Location: Brazil
- Key details: Doctors told her both she and the baby would die. She prayed to Gianna. The bleeding stopped, and both mother and child survived healthy.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (2004)
