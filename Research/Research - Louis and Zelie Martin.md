Miracles Project - Research: Louis and Zelie Martin

Basic Info:
- Louis: August 22, 1823, Bordeaux, France → Died: July 29, 1894
- Zelie: December 23, 1831, Gandelain, France → Died: August 28, 1877
- Beatified: October 19, 2008
- Canonized: October 18, 2015 (by Pope Francis)
- Feast Day: July 12
- Patronage: Married couples, families, parents, Catholic families
- Note: First married couple to be canonized together (not just beatified)

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:

- Key details: The healing was prayed for through the intercession of Louis and Zelie Martin
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable

- Date of healing: 2012 or 2013
- Location: Italy
- Key details: The infant's parents prayed to the Martins. The baby recovered fully and doctors could not explain it.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (2015)
