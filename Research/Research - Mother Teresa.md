Miracles Project - Research: Mother Teresa

Basic Info:
- Born: Anjeze Gonxhe Bojaxhiu, August 26, 1910, Skopje (now North Macedonia)
- Died: September 5, 1997
- Beatified: October 19, 2003 (by John Paul II)
- Canonized: September 4, 2016 (by Pope Francis)
- Feast Day: September 5
- Patronage: Missionaries of Charity, poor and sick

KNOWN MIRACLES ATTRIBUTED TO HER INTERCESSION:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Monica Besra, a Bengali woman
- Condition: Abdominal tumor (large, benignant ovarian tumor)
- Date of healing: September 5, 1998 (exactly one year after Mother Teresa's death)
- Location: Nadia district, West Bengal, India
- Key details: She had a large tumor causing severe pain and immobility. After applying a small medallion of Mother Teresa and praying, she felt intense warmth and the tumor disappeared.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 2002
- Used for: Beatification (2003)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Marcilio Haddad Andrino, a Brazilian mechanical engineer
- Condition: Multiple brain abscesses caused by bacterial infection (viral meningoencephalitis with hydrocephalus)
- Date of healing: December 9, 2008
- Location: Santos, Brazil
- Key details: He was in a coma, on life support, with family told to prepare for his death. His wife prayed to Mother Teresa with a relic. Doctors found him conscious the next morning with all symptoms gone.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 2015
- Used for: Canonization (2016)
