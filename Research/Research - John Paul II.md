Miracles Project - Research: John Paul II

Basic Info:
- Born: Karol Jozef Wojtyla, May 18, 1920, Wadowice, Poland
- Died: April 2, 2005
- Canonized: April 27, 2014 (Divine Mercy Sunday)
- Canonized by: Pope Francis
- Feast Day: October 22
- Patronage: World Youth Day, families, young Catholics

KNOWN MIRACLES ATTRIBUTED TO HIS INTERCESSION:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Sr. Marie Simon-Pierre, a French nun
- Condition: Parkinson's disease (diagnosed 2001)
- Date of healing: June 2, 2005 (2 months after JPII's death)
- Location: Aix-en-Provence, France
- Key details: She had severe Parkinson's - could not write or drive. On the night of June 2, she felt compelled to write JPII's name and found her hand steady.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 2009
- Used for: Beatification (May 1, 2011)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Floribeth Mora Diaz, a Costa Rican woman
- Condition: Severe brain aneurysm (diagnosed April 2011)
- Date of healing: May 1, 2011 (day of JPII's beatification)
- Location: Costa Rica
- Key details: She was terminal, sent home to die. While watching JPII's beatification on TV, she heard his voice say "Don't be afraid. I am with you." She felt immediate improvement.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 2013
- Used for: Canonization (April 27, 2014)
