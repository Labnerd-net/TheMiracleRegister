Miracles Project - Research: Andre Bessette

Basic Info:
- Born: August 9, 1845, Mont-Saint-Gregoire, Quebec, Canada
- Died: January 6, 1937, Montreal, Quebec, Canada
- Beatified: May 23, 1982 (by John Paul II)
- Canonized: October 17, 2010 (by Benedict XVI)
- Feast Day: January 6
- Patronage: The sick, the grieving, family caregivers

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Joseph Audet, a Canadian man
- Condition: Severe tuberculosis (considered incurable, given up on by doctors)
- Date of healing: Early 1920s (during Brother Andre's lifetime)
- Location: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
- Key details: Joseph prayed at St. Joseph's Oratory and applied oil from a lamp that burned at St. Joseph's statue. Brother Andre blessed him and he recovered.
- Used for: Beatification (1982)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Mario De Montigny, a 10-year-old boy
- Condition: Severe tarsal fracture to his foot that would not heal properly (after an ATV accident)
- Date of healing: March 13, 1991
- Location: Montreal, Quebec, Canada
- Key details: Doctors said he would have a permanent limp or require amputation. Mario's family prayed to Brother Andre and applied blessed oil from St. Joseph's Oratory. The foot healed completely and normally.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (2010)

ADDITIONAL CONTEXT:
- During his lifetime, Brother Andre was known for thousands of healings at St. Joseph's Oratory
- Often called "The Miracle Man of Montreal"
- The Oratory has rooms filled with crutches and canes left by those healed
