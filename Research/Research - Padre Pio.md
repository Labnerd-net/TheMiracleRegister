Miracles Project - Research: Padre Pio

Basic Info:
- Born: Francesco Forgione, May 25, 1887, Pietrelcina, Italy
- Died: September 23, 1968
- Canonized: June 16, 2002 (by John Paul II)
- Feast Day: September 23
- Patronage: Stress relief, civil defense volunteers, adolescents

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Consiglia De Martino, a young Italian woman
- Condition: Ruptured stomach / severe peritonitis from burst appendix
- Date of healing: Morning of October 7, 1958
- Location: Salerno, Italy
- Key details: She was near death after botched surgery. Her father brought a photo of Padre Pio to her hospital room and prayed. She saw a vision of a Capuchin friar who said "Don't you know I only work at night?" She was healed instantly.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Beatification (May 2, 1999)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Matteo Pio Colella, a boy from San Giovanni Rotondo
- Condition: Coma after meningitis, severe brain damage, multiple organ failure
- Date of healing: July 21, 2000
- Location: San Giovanni Rotondo, Italy
- Key details: The 7-year-old was dying. His mother prayed intensely at Padre Pio's tomb. He recovered fully with no brain damage.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (2002)

ADDITIONAL WELL-ATTRIBUTED MIRACLES (not used for canonization):
