Miracles Project - Research: Carlo Acutis

Basic Info:
- Born: May 3, 1991, London, England (raised in Milan, Italy)
- Died: October 12, 2006, Monza, Italy (leukemia)
- Beatified: October 10, 2020 (by Cardinal Agostino Vallini, Assisi)
- Canonization: Expected / forthcoming
- Feast Day: October 12
- Patronage: Computer programmers, internet users, youth, Eucharistic devotion
- Key detail: Created the Eucharistic Miracles exhibition website that is still active

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Matheus (a young boy from Campo Grande, Brazil, surname Vianna or similar)
- Condition: Rare pancreatic disease (possibly annular pancreas or related congenital condition)
- Date of healing: 2010s
- Location: Campo Grande, Brazil
- Key details: The boy's mother prayed to Carlo after learning about his story. Doctors had said surgery was the only option. The boy recovered spontaneously.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Beatification (2020)

Miracle 2 - Second miracle (for canonization, reported confirmed):
- Recipient: A young girl from Costa Rica
- Condition: Severe head trauma / brain injury from a fall
- Date of healing: 2022
- Location: Costa Rica
- Key details: Details are emerging as the Vatican confirms the miracle for canonization
- Used for: Canonization (expected soon)
