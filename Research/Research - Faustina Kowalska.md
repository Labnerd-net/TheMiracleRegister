Miracles Project - Research: Faustina Kowalska

Basic Info:
- Born: Helena Kowalska, August 25, 1905, Glogowiec, Poland
- Died: October 5, 1938, Krakow
- Beatified: April 18, 1993 (by John Paul II)
- Canonized: April 30, 2000 (by John Paul II)
- Feast Day: October 5
- Patronage: Divine Mercy devotion, sinners, world youth day

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Maureen Digan, a woman from Massachusetts, USA
- Condition: Lymphedema (chronic, severe swelling of legs, unable to walk)
- Date of healing: March 13, 1981
- Location: Fall River, Massachusetts, USA
- Key details: She had suffered for 36 years. While visiting St. Faustina's tomb in Poland, she prayed and felt a cracking sensation. The swelling drained immediately and she could walk.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 1992
- Used for: Beatification (1993)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Fr. Ronald Pytel, a Polish-American priest
- Condition: Severe heart condition (advanced arteriosclerosis, angina)
- Date of healing: 1995
- Location: Baltimore, Maryland, USA
- Key details: He was told he needed bypass surgery. After praying to Faustina, his symptoms disappeared and medical tests showed a normal heart.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable in 1999
- Used for: Canonization (2000)
