Miracles Project - Research: Kateri Tekakwitha

Basic Info:
- Born: 1656, Ossernenon (now Auriesville, New York)
- Died: April 17, 1680, Kahnawake, Quebec, Canada
- Beatified: June 22, 1980 (by John Paul II)
- Canonized: October 21, 2012 (by Benedict XVI)
- Feast Day: July 14 (USA and Canada)
- Patronage: Native Americans, ecology, environmentalists, exiles

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: A Native American boy (identity kept private at the time)
- Condition: Severe illness (records not publicly detailed in English)
- Date of healing: 1940s-1950s timeframe
- Location: United States (specific location not publicly detailed)
- Key details: Healed through prayer to Kateri
- Used for: Beatification (1980)

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Jake Finkbonner, a 6-year-old boy
- Condition: Necrotizing fasciitis (flesh-eating bacteria) from a facial injury
- Date of healing: Early 2006
- Location: Ferndale, Washington, USA
- Key details: The infection was spreading rapidly, doctors said he would die or require extensive facial disfigurement. A relic of Kateri was applied and prayers offered by his family and the local Native American community. The infection stopped and he fully recovered without significant scarring.
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (2012)
