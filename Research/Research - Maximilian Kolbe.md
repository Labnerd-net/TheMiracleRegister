Miracles Project - Research: Maximilian Kolbe

Basic Info:
- Born: January 8, 1894, Zdunska Wola, Poland
- Died: August 14, 1941, Auschwitz, Poland (martyr)
- Beatified: October 17, 1971 (by Paul VI)
- Canonized: October 10, 1982 (by John Paul II)
- Feast Day: August 14
- Patronage: Addicts, journalists, prisoners, families, pro-life movement
- Canonization type: Martyr (therefore only 1 miracle required for canonization, but 2 were confirmed)

KNOWN MIRACLES:

Miracle 1 - Beatification Miracle:
- Recipient: Maria Gąsior, a Polish woman
- Condition: Intestinal tuberculosis (advanced, considered terminal)
- Date of healing: 1940s or 1950s
- Location: Poland
- Key details: Prayed to Maximilian Kolbe for intercession
- Used for: Beatification (1971)
- Note: As a martyr, only needed 1 miracle for beatification

Miracle 2 - Canonization Miracle:
- Recipient: Angela Testoni, an Italian woman
- Condition: Calcified peritoneum / severe intestinal disease (extensive calcificatio peritonealis)
- Date of healing: 1950s or 1960s
- Location: Italy
- Key details: Healing attributed to Kolbe's intercession
- Vatican medical board: Declared medically inexplicable
- Used for: Canonization (1982)
- Note: As a martyr, technically dispensed from a second miracle, but one was confirmed anyway
