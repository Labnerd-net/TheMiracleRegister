Canonization Miracle Status: Blessed Stanley Rother

No Miracle record exists for Blessed Stanley Rother because no canonization-track
miracle case is publicly documented yet. This file exists in place of a
"Miracle 1" writeup to make that explicit, rather than leaving it to be
inferred or guessed at later.

---

Why there's no beatification miracle either (and why that's different):
Rother's beatification required no miracle at all. The Congregation (now
Dicastery) for the Causes of Saints confirmed on December 1, 2016 that he
died a martyr — in odium fidei — and martyrs are canonically dispensed from
the usual beatification-miracle requirement. That's recorded on the Data.md
`saints` row as `beatification_miracle_dispensed: true`,
`dispensation_reason: martyr`.

That dispensation does NOT extend to canonization. Even a recognized martyr
still needs one verified miracle attributed to their intercession, occurring
after beatification, before the Church can canonize them. For Rother, that
miracle has not yet been identified/confirmed publicly. So:
`canonization_miracle_dispensed` should be recorded as `false` — this is a
pending requirement, not a waived one. The "1 miracle only" shorthand in
`context/Notes/Priority Additions.md` refers to this outstanding requirement,
not to one already in hand.

---

What is publicly known about the search for a canonization miracle (as of
October 2026, via Catholic press coverage — no Vatican dicastery decree or
diocesan tribunal document has named a specific case):

- The Archdiocese of Oklahoma City's cause continues to be actively
  shepherded. Deacon Norm Mejstrik, who led the case-building work since
  2007, was reported in 2026 as stepping back from that part of his role;
  Maria Scaperlanda — author of the Rother biography "The Shepherd Who
  Didn't Run" — is taking on a larger role in the cause going forward.
- The Rome-based postulator for the cause, Cristiana Marinelli, is quoted
  (via Catholic press coverage) as saying there are "active investigations"
  into candidate healings attributed to Rother's intercession, but she
  declined to provide case specifics publicly.
- No recipient name, diagnosis, location, or date has been published for any
  candidate case. There is nothing to write up as a Miracle record — doing
  so would mean fabricating details that don't exist in any source.

If/when the Archdiocese of Oklahoma City or the Dicastery for the Causes of
Saints names a specific case under investigation (or confirms one), replace
this file with a proper "Miracle 1 - [Title].md" following the standard
