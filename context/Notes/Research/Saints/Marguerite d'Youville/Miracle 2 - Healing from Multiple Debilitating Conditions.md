STATUS: Unconfirmed - do not publish without further archival research

This stub originally assumed a second miracle existed for Marguerite d'Youville
beyond the leukemia/prolonged-remission case documented in
`Miracle 1 - Healing of a Woman from Leukemia.md`. That assumption was likely
based on pre-1983 canon law, under which beatification required two miracles
reviewed before the beatification itself (hers was May 3, 1959) and
canonization required further miracles after beatification. Under the
simplified 1983 code, Pope John Paul II's canonizations (including d'Youville's
in 1990) required only a single post-beatification miracle - which is the
leukemia case already written up as Miracle 1.

## What was checked (2026-10-05)

Extensive English- and French-language web research across Vatican sources,
academic literature (Dictionary of Canadian Biography, CMAJ, Jacalyn Duffin's
own writing), the Grey Nuns of Montreal's own site, Quebec diocesan history
pages, and general press turned up no corroborated second miracle - neither a
distinct healing used for the 1990 canonization, nor the miracle(s) that would
have been reviewed ahead of her 1959 beatification. One AI-search synthesis
surfaced an unsourced claim that "two miracles were recognized in 1957" (which
would track as the pre-beatification miracles under the old law), but no
underlying document or secondary source corroborated it on a direct fetch -
treat that claim as unconfirmed.

The Dictionary of Canadian Biography entry (Claudette Lacelle, 1979 - written
before both the Duffin case and the 1990 canonization) only notes that
"tradition attributes miracles... to her," with no specifics. No 1957-59 era
decree, Positio excerpt, or contemporary (pre-1959) newspaper account of a
specific healing was locatable through general web search.

## Why this likely needs offline/archival sources, not more web search

The beatification miracle(s), if distinctly documented at all, would most
likely appear in:
- The Positio for her cause (held in the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints'
  own archive in Rome - not published, per the project's established
  position on primary medical/causa records, see `Source Requirements
  Standard.md`)
- Contemporary 1950s Quebec Catholic press (not digitized/indexed in a way
  general web search surfaces)
- A full scholarly biography of d'Youville, if one exists, that predates or
  covers the 1959 process in detail

## Recommendation

Don't publish a second miracle record for d'Youville on invented or
unconfirmed details. Options:
1. Publish the saint with only the one well-sourced miracle (the leukemia
