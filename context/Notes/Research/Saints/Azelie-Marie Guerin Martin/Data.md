Cure Details:             Carmen was born extremely prematurely (~26 weeks) in Valencia, four days before the beatification of Louis and Zélie Martin (October 19, 2008). She suffered a grade 4 intraventricular hemorrhage — the most severe classification — and was expected to die or survive with serious disabilities. Her family prayed to the newly beatified Louis and Zélie through the Barefoot Carmelite nuns at Serra. She gradually and completely recovered. Eight doctors testified there was no scientific explanation. The miracle was accepted by the Congregation for the Causes of Saints on March 18, 2015.
