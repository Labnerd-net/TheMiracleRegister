Synopsis: Canonization Miracle of Bernadette Soubirous

Saint:        Bernadette Soubirous
Miracle:      Healing of Sister Marie de Saint-Fidèle
Recipient:    Sister Marie de Saint-Fidèle
Condition:    Pott's disease (tuberculosis of the spine)
Date:         Between 1925–1928 (within three years of beatification; exact date not in public record)
Location:     Unknown

---

Note: The Church certified two miracles for Bernadette's canonization. The other was the healing of Archbishop Lemaître of Carthage from a chronic amoebic infection. Sister Marie de Saint-Fidèle is documented here as the primary canonization miracle record.

---

Sister Marie de Saint-Fidèle suffered from Pott's disease — tuberculosis of the spine, one of the most debilitating manifestations of mycobacterial infection. The bacteria destroy vertebral bone over time, collapsing the spinal column, producing the characteristic angular deformity known as Pott's kyphosis, and frequently causing severe, incapacitating pain or paralysis. In the late 1920s there was no effective treatment; streptomycin would not reach clinical use for another two decades. Cases as advanced as hers typically ended in permanent disability or death.

Following Bernadette's beatification on June 14, 1925, Sister Marie de Saint-Fidèle attributed her recovery to Bernadette's intercession. The healing was declared complete, sudden, and medically inexplicable by examining physicians. The case was submitted to the Congregation of Rites as part of the formal canonization process. Two miracles were required; this case, alongside the healing of Archbishop Lemaître of Carthage from a chronic amoebic infection, met that threshold. The decree de tuto — the final step in the canonization process — was published July 2, 1933. Pope Pius XI canonized Bernadette on December 8, 1933, the Feast of the Immaculate Conception, at St. Peter's Basilica.
