Synopsis: Beatification Miracle of Bernadette Soubirous

Saint:        Bernadette Soubirous
Miracle:      Healing of Sister Marie-Mélanie Meyer
Recipient:    Sister Marie-Mélanie Meyer (Sisters of Providence of Ribeauvillé)
Condition:    Gastric ulcer — near-fatal, unable to take nourishment
Date:         Unknown (onset 1910; cure certified by Church May 2, 1925)
Location:     Convent of Saint-Gildard, Nevers, France (pilgrimage to Bernadette's tomb)

---

Note: The Church certified two miracles for Bernadette's beatification on May 2, 1925. The other was Henri Boisselet, cured of tubercular peritonitis via novena. Sister Marie-Mélanie Meyer is documented here as the primary beatification miracle record.

---

Sister Marie-Mélanie Meyer served as infirmarian at the Sacred Heart Convent in Moulins, France, under the Sisters of Providence of Ribeauvillé. In 1910, when she was thirty years old, she began experiencing acute abdominal pain and frequent, persistent vomiting. A gastric ulcer was diagnosed. Over the following years her condition worsened progressively: she became unable to keep down any nourishment, was reduced to extreme weakness, and her physicians regarded her situation as near hopeless.

Sometime before 1925 — the exact year is not in the public record — Sister Meyer made a pilgrimage to the tomb of Bernadette Soubirous at the Convent of Saint-Gildard in Nevers. There she prayed for Bernadette's intercession. Following this visit she experienced a complete recovery from the ulcer that had disabled her for years. Her physicians found no natural explanation for the cure.

The case was submitted to the Church's formal process for Bernadette's beatification. The Congregation of Rites examined ten proposed miracles in total and selected two for verification. Sister Meyer's cure was certified on May 2, 1925, alongside the cure of Henri Boisselet from tubercular peritonitis. Six weeks later, on June 14, 1925, Pope Pius XI beatified Bernadette Soubirous in Rome.

KEY NOTES:
- Exact date of cure not in public record; condition onset 1910
- intercessory_medium: tomb_prayer (direct pilgrimage to Bernadette's tomb at Saint-Gildard)
- One of two beatification miracles; the other (Henri Boisselet, tubercular peritonitis, cured via novena) is not separately recorded here
