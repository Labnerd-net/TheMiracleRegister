Born January 9, 1902, in Barbastro, Spain. Ordained priest in 1925.

Founded Opus Dei in 1928 — a Catholic organization teaching that everyone is called to holiness through ordinary work and daily life.

His spiritual classic 'The Way' sold millions of copies. A controversial figure in some circles but widely influential in 20th-century Catholic spirituality.

Beatified by John Paul II on May 17, 1992. Canonized by John Paul II on October 6, 2002.
