Biography: John XXIII

[TO BE WRITTEN — ~300 words]

Born Angelo Giuseppe Roncalli on November 25, 1881, in Sotto il Monte, Bergamo, Italy. Pope from 1958-1963.

Priest, bishop, papal diplomat in Bulgaria, Turkey, and France. Patriarch of Venice before being elected Pope.

Convened the Second Vatican Council (1962-1965), a watershed event in modern Church history. Known for his warmth, humor, and pastoral approach.

Beatified by John Paul II on September 3, 2000. Canonized by Pope Francis on April 27, 2014 (dispensed from 2nd miracle requirement).
