Feast Day:           March 24
Feast Scope:         national — scopeDetail: El Salvador. No evidence found of a specific Dicastery for Divine Worship decree inscribing Romero into the General Roman Calendar (contrast with Paul VI, who received such a decree Jan 25, 2019 — https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/ccdds/documents/rc_con_ccdds_doc_20190125_decreto-celebrazione-paolovi_en.html). Every canonized saint is automatically added to the Roman Martyrology on their dies natalis, and Vatican News's "Saint of the Day" feature (not evidence of universal-calendar status) does list him March 24. He is widely celebrated in El Salvador and across Latin America/missionary dioceses as a local/national optional memorial. Flagging this distinction rather than guessing "universal."

Dispensation Reason: martyr — applies to the beatification leg only. As a martyr, Romero was dispensed from the usual beatification miracle requirement entirely (zero miracles needed for beatification). Canonization for a martyr still requires one miracle (vs. the usual two for non-martyrs) — that single miracle WAS required and WAS supplied (see Miracle 1 file). Flagging this explicitly because the project's "Priority Additions" note ("1 miracle (beatification)") appears to mis-describe which track the one on-file miracle belongs to — research found no evidence of a separate beatification-track miracle; the one documented miracle is the canonization miracle (Vatican decree dated March 6, 2018, more than two years after the May 2015 beatification). Open question carried into the Miracle 1 file.
