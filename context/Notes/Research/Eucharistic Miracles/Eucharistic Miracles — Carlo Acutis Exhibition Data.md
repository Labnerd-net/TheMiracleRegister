## Notable Miracles

- **Lanciano, Italy (750 AD)** — The oldest well-documented Eucharistic miracle. Host turned to visible flesh (cardiac tissue) and wine to blood (type AB). Still visible today.
- **Bolsena/Orvieto, Italy (1264)** — Bleeding host during Mass of a doubting priest; led Pope Urban IV to institute the Feast of Corpus Christi.
- **Siena, Italy (1730)** — 223 consecrated hosts remained incorrupt for over 200 years after being stolen. Tested by science in 1914 and 1922.
- **Santarém, Portugal (1247)** — Bleeding host, visible to this day.
- **Tixtla, Mexico (2006)** — Modern miracle; host turned to cardiac tissue, scientifically analyzed.
- **Sokółka, Poland (2008)** — Host turned to cardiac tissue; medical analysis confirmed it was a living human heart muscle in agonal state.
- **Legnica, Poland (2013)** — Host turned to cardiac tissue; analysis confirmed it was from a dying person.
