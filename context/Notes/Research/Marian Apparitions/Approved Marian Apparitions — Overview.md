## Notes

- Belief in private revelations (even approved ones) is not required of the faithful — they are "worthy of belief" but not Articles of Faith.
- The local bishop is the first authority in judging apparitions (Council of Trent, 1545-63). Vatican approval is not required for an apparition to be considered authentic.
- No positive judgment by a bishop has ever been reversed in Church history.
- Negative judgments ("Non constat de supernaturalitate") have occasionally been later reversed to positive.
- There are also approximately 200-300+ unapproved apparition claims worldwide (Medjugorje being the most prominent).
