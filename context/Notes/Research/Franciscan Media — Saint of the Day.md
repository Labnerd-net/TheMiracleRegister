# Franciscan Media — Saint of the Day

**Website:** https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/
**Alphabetical index:** https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/sort/
**Calendar view:** https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/calendar/

Each saint has a dedicated page with a short biography, feast day, and patronage. URL pattern:
`https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/[saint-slug]/`

Example: https://www.franciscanmedia.org/saint-of-the-day/saint-elizabeth-ann-seton/

Use as a reference for biography drafts, feast days, and candidate saints to add to the DB.

## Known Date Discrepancies

FM's feast dates do not always match official Catholic calendars. See `Catholic Feast Day Reference.md` for the authoritative list. Do not use FM as the source of truth for feast dates.
