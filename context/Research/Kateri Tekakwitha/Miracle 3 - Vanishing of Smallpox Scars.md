Saint:        Kateri Tekakwitha
Miracle Type: associated (visible transformation)
Event:        Facial scars vanished immediately after death
Date:         April 17, 1680
Location:     Kahnawake, Quebec, Canada

Key Narrative Points:
- Kateri survived smallpox epidemic at age 4, left with facial scarring and impaired vision
- Spent her life with visible scars
- At the moment of her death, witnesses observed the scars vanish
- Her skin became smooth and beautiful
- Multiple witnesses present at her deathbed testified to this
- Phenomenon cited in her canonization process
- Name "Tekakwitha" means "she who gropes her way" (due to impaired vision)
- Born, lived, and died in obscurity, now first Native American saint

Sources:
- Vatican Positio documents
- Contemporary witness accounts
