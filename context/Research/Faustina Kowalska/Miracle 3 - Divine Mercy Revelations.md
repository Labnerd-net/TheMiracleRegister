Saint:        Faustina Kowalska
Miracle Type: associated (apparition)
Event:        Visions of Jesus Christ
Period:       1931-1938
Location:     Krakow and Vilnius, Poland

Key Narrative Points:
- First vision: February 22, 1931 in her cell at Plock
- Jesus appeared with red and white rays from His heart
- Instructed her to paint the image with the words "Jesus, I trust in You"
- Dictated the Chaplet of Divine Mercy (1935)
- Established Divine Mercy Sunday (Sunday after Easter)
- Diary spans 600+ pages documenting 7 years of visions
- Diary initially condemned, later approved by Vatican
- Divine Mercy devotion spread worldwide by John Paul II
- Feast of Divine Mercy Sunday established by Vatican in 2000
