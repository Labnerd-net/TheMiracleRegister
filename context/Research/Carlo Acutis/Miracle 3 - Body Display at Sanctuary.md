Saint:        Carlo Acutis
Miracle Type: associated (wax-encased body display)
Event:        Exhumation and public display of remains
Exhumation:   2007
Public Display: 2020
Location:     Sanctuary of Santa Maria Maggiore, Assisi, Italy

Key Narrative Points:
- Carlo died of leukemia on October 12, 2006, age 15
- Body exhumed in 2007 for transfer to Assisi
- Found to have undergone normal decay per Archbishop Sorrentino
- Described as "fully integral" (complete skeleton) but not intact
- Displayed in glass tomb with wax layer molded to resemble living appearance
- Common Italian tradition (also used for St. John XXIII, St. Julian Eymard)
- Wears tracksuit and sneakers reflecting his ordinary youth
- NOT declared incorrupt by the Church (despite popular online claims)
- His heart is preserved separately as a relic at the sanctuary
- His Eucharistic miracle website (miracolieucaristici.org) remains active

- Aleteia (Philip Kosloski, Oct 2023)
